  LedgerLoop — the AI finance-controller for Indian shopkeepers
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

LedgerLoop is a friendly "finance controller" that lives in your pocket
and does the bookkeeping your CA usually nags you about. You just send
it your bills — a photo from your phone, a PDF, a WhatsApp-style forward
— and it reads them, files them correctly, flags anything suspicious,
and hands your CA clean, GST-ready registers at month-end. It's built
for India, for Indian GST, and for shopkeepers who don't want to be
accountants.

It was built for Razorpay's AI Buildathon (Track 4: AI Finance Controller).


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
1. WHAT IT DOES FOR YOU — THE WHOLE FLOW
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

    Snap / send a bill  ──▶  LedgerLoop reads it  ──▶
    You glance & approve ──▶  It's filed cleanly    ──▶
    Month-end: your CA gets two ready-to-file registers

Every bill you ever get — purchase, sale, GST or tax-free, messy or
clean — ends up in ONE simple ledger. Nothing gets lost, nothing gets
filed without your say-so.

How you add a bill (your choice):
  • Telegram: forward a photo or PDF to your bot in one message.
  • Web: drop it on the upload page in your dashboard.

What happens next, automatically:
  1. The app reads the bill — even bad camera photos are OCR'd so
     print stays readable.
  2. It extracts the important fields: who the party is (supplier or
     customer), bill number, date, GSTIN, taxable amount, tax split,
     total, category, and the HSN codes of the goods.
  3. It checks everything at once (see "catches problems" below).
  4. Clean bills go straight into the ledger; anything doubtful is
     parked with a plain-language reason so you can decide quickly.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
2. EVERYTHING IT DOES — THE FEATURES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

A. BILL INTAKE THAT ACTUALLY WORKS ON INDIAN BILLS
   • Send bills by Telegram photo, PDF, or web upload.
   • Camera photos are enhanced and OCR'd, so scans don't need to be
     perfect — a phone pic of a kirana bill works.
   • Every filed bill keeps its original file — tap to view the actual
     bill anytime and double-check the numbers, right from the ledger.
   • Bills are filed under the right month automatically from the date
     on the bill (Indian day/month/year style is understood).

B. GST, DONE THE INDIAN WAY
   • One default GST rate for your whole shop — you pick your business
     type (clothing, groceries, pharmacy, stationery, restaurant,
     electronics, hardware, general store) and the app sets the right
     rate, which you can edit anytime.
   • Rates follow the current Government rules (GST 2.0, in force since
     22 Sep 2025): 5% merit rate for most retail goods, 18% standard
     otherwise. Clothing up to ₹2,500 per piece is 5%.
   • When a bill shows only a lump total with no tax printed, the app
     works out CGST + SGST (same state) or IGST (inter-state) for you —
     and always shows a small note on the entry explaining why that tax
     was calculated.
   • Purchases NEVER get fake input-tax invented — if a bill doesn't
     show tax, it stays tax-free with a "please verify" note.
   • A CA-ready report keeps per-item HSN/SAC codes from every bill.

C. IT CATCHES PROBLEMS BEFORE YOUR CA DOES
   The app triple-checks every single bill and flags anything odd in
   plain language:
   • Duplicate bill — same supplier and bill number already filed.
   • Invalid GSTIN — checked against the official format AND the
     government's mod-36 checksum, not just "looks about right".
   • Tax mismatch — taxable + CGST + SGST + IGST doesn't equal the
     total.
   • Missing or invalid HSN/SAC code (needed for filing).
   • Wrong tax treatment — intra-state tax where it should be
     inter-state, or vice versa.
   • Unreadable date or missing fields.
   • Hard failures (unreadable file, service down) are also parked —
     nothing ever silently vanishes.
   Every flagged bill has a button to review, correct, approve, or
   dismiss — from your Telegram chat OR the dashboard.

D. REVIEW IN SECONDS, RIGHT IN TELEGRAM
   • Approve / Dismiss / View buttons appear right under the flagged
     bill in your chat — no need to open the web app.
   • /audit shows your recent activity; /monthend sends the month-end
     email on your behalf.

E. MONTH-END FOR YOUR CA — ONE TAP, CLEAN OUTPUT
   • Press "Send to CA" and your CA receives an email with two
     attachments and a short note. That's it — no 40-page summary.
   • Purchases register (CSV) and Sales register (CSV), in the format
     the GST ecosystem expects (the same shape as GSTR-1 / GSTR-2B).
   • One row per bill — date (DD-MM-YYYY), party name, bill number,
     party GSTIN, invoice value, taxable value, and CGST/SGST/IGST.
   • Both files are attached every month, even if one register is
     empty (so your CA sees a clean zero, not a missing file).
   • You can also download the two CSVs yourself from the dashboard,
     plus a full-detail JSON backup of everything.

F. YOUR DASHBOARD
   • Ledger — every filed bill for any month, expand a row to see the
     tax note and details.
   • Exceptions — everything flagged, with reasons, ready to review.
   • Send — preview exactly what your CA will receive, then send.
   • Audit — a tamper-proof history of every edit and send.
   • Settings — business type, GST rate, your state (for intra/
     inter-state GST), GST registration on/off, and your CA's email.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
3. WHAT YOUR CA RECEIVES (AND WHY IT'S FILE-READY)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

At month-end the CA gets an email with:

  Subject:  [LedgerLoop] 2026-08 registers for CA review
  Body:     A few lines — these are the purchase & sales registers for
            the month, auto-extracted and pending your review; no
            returns have been filed.

  Attachments:
    • purchases-2026-08.csv   — Purchase register
        GSTIN of Supplier │ Supplier Name │ Invoice Number │ Invoice
        Date │ Invoice Value │ Taxable Value │ CGST │ SGST │ IGST
    • sales-2026-08.csv       — Sales register
        GSTIN of Recipient │ Customer Name │ Invoice Number │ Invoice
        Date │ Place of Supply │ Invoice Value │ Taxable Value │
        CGST │ SGST │ IGST

That's exactly the format used for GST-return reconciliation, so your
CA can drop the files straight into their tools. Every entry says who
the party is, the bill number, the value, and the tax split — one row
per bill, nothing more, nothing missing.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
4. TRUST & SAFETY — NON-NEGOTIABLES
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   • LedgerLoop NEVER files anything. No returns, no approvals, no
     auto-payment. Sending to your CA is always you pressing the button.
   • Nothing is ever silently dropped — every unreadable bill lands in
     exceptions with a reason.
   • Auto-calculated tax always carries a visible "why" note, so you
     (and your CA) can tell computed from printed.
   • Every edit, approve, dismiss, and send is written to an audit log
     — a clean history if your CA asks "where did this come from?".
   • Figures are labelled "auto-extracted — pending CA review", not
     final filing data.
   • Emails go out through your own Google account token (no shared
     mail server reading your data), and a dry-run guard means nothing
     leaves the machine until you configure sending.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5. BUILT FOR INDIA, FULLY
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   • Indian GST states and state codes (Karnataka 29, Maharashtra 27, …)
   • Intra-state → CGST + SGST; inter-state → IGST
   • GSTIN validation with the official mod-36 checksum
   • Indian date format on exports (DD-MM-YYYY)
   • HSN/SAC codes for goods
   • ₹ amounts, GST 2.0 slabs (5% / 18%)
   • Local kirana realities: phone-photo bills, lump totals with no tax
     printed, ink that smudges — all handled.


────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────
6. QUICK START (USING IT, NOT SETTING IT UP)
────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

   1. Open the dashboard (or just open your Telegram chat with the bot).
   2. Send your first bill — a photo or PDF works.
   3. It files itself; if it's flagged, tap Approve or Dismiss.
   4. Browse the ledger — every bill is there with its tax split.
   5. Download "Purchases (CSV)" and "Sales (CSV)" for your own records.
   6. At month-end, hit "Send to CA" — your CA gets the two registers
      with a short note.

That's the whole job. Send a bill, glance, approve, done — and in the
background your books stay correct, audited, and GST-ready for when
your CA asks.
